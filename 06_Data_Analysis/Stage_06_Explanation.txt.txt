STAGE 6: DATA ANALYSIS

Purpose:
To analyze the aggregated financial data and identify important spending, income, savings, and relationship patterns.

Input:
1. expense_by_category.csv
2. monthly_summary.csv

Operations:
1. Calculate descriptive statistics for expense categories.
2. Identify the category with the highest total spending.
3. Identify the month with the highest total expense.
4. Identify the month with the highest total income.
5. Identify the month with the highest savings.
6. Calculate the correlation between monthly income and monthly expenses.

Analysis Outputs:
- Descriptive statistics
- Highest spending category
- Highest expense month
- Highest income month
- Highest savings month
- Income-expense correlation

Purpose of Correlation:
Correlation is used to measure the relationship between monthly income and monthly expenses.

Output:
Important financial patterns and statistical results are identified for visualization and interpretation in the next stages.

data_analysis.py
