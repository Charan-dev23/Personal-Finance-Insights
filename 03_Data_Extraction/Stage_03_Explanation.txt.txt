STAGE 3: DATA EXTRACTION

Purpose:
To convert the transaction date into a proper date format and extract useful time-related information for analysis.

Input:
Filtered expense and income transaction data.

Operations:
1. Convert the date_time column into a date format.
2. Extract the year from date_time.
3. Extract the month number from date_time.
4. Extract the month name from date_time.

Derived Fields:
- year
- month
- month_name

Example:
A transaction dated in November 2025 can be represented as:
Year: 2025
Month: 11
Month Name: November

Purpose of Derived Fields:
The extracted year and month information is used later to calculate monthly income, monthly expenses, and savings.

Output:
Transaction data with additional date-based fields ready for validation, cleaning, and further analysis.
