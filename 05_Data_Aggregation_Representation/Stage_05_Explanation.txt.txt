STAGE 5: DATA AGGREGATION & REPRESENTATION

Purpose:
To summarize the cleaned transaction data into useful financial groups and calculate monthly financial measures.

Input:
1. cleaned_expenses.csv
2. cleaned_income.csv

Operations:
1. Group expenses by category and calculate the total amount.
2. Group expenses by year and month.
3. Group income by year and month.
4. Combine monthly income and expense data.
5. Calculate monthly savings.

Aggregated Outputs:
- expense_by_category
- expense_by_month
- income_by_month
- monthly_summary

Derived Fields in monthly_summary:
- total_income
- total_expense
- savings

Calculations:
Total Income = Sum of income transactions for each month.
Total Expense = Sum of expense transactions for each month.
Savings = Total Income - Total Expense

Output Files:
- expense_by_category.csv
- expense_by_month.csv
- income_by_month.csv
- monthly_summary.csv

data_aggregation.py
